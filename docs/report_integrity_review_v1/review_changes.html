How to use the 20% caps

Obtain an authorized institutional draft check and both reports. Record the scanned file's identity and the report settings. Ask the guide how the department treats bibliography, legitimate quotes, standard front matter, prior stored drafts and the AI indicator's asterisk. Use the institution's settings consistently rather than changing exclusions to obtain a desired result.

Turnitin's AI report currently suppresses numerical scores above zero and below 20%, using an asterisk. That is not zero and does not replace the institution's acceptance decision. A numerical 20% is at your stated maximum, but the official wording and interpretation should govern the submission. Official explanation

For similarity findings, inspect the actual passages: quote and attribute exact borrowed wording, cite borrowed ideas, and remove unsupported material. Standard institutional wording should be handled under the approved template rules. For AI flags, review the prose and discuss the permitted use of assistance with the guide. Citations and an assistance disclosure improve attribution and transparency; neither guarantees a lower AI score.
